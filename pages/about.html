The EventSphere Difference

Say Goodbye to the Old Way of Ticketing

Traditional event management in Sri Lanka has long relied on manual bank slips and social media DMs. Here is how EventSphere transforms the experience.

The Old Frustration

  • Manual Bank Slips: Attendees must make transfers, take screenshots, and wait 24–48 hours for someone to confirm.
  • Accidental Overselling: Multiple buyers purchase the same seats during high-demand rushes because spreadsheets lag behind.
  • Ticket Duplication & Fraud: Static PDF tickets get forwarded or copied on WhatsApp, causing heartbreaking gate denials.
  • Gate Queues: Door staff check names against printed paper lists with highlighters in dark concert venues.

The EventSphere Standard

  • Instant Digital Confirmation: Pay via Visa, Mastercard, or Genie on PayHere and receive tickets instantly.
  • Guaranteed Seat Reservations: 10-minute cart hold locks your tickets while you pay, guaranteeing zero double-bookings.
  • Cryptographic Anti-Tamper Passes: Each QR pass is uniquely signed and sent directly to individual attendees.
  • High-Speed Gate Scanner: Door staff use any smartphone camera to check in attendees with dual audio-visual feedback.

Under The Hood

Core Technical Systems & Engineering Highlights

Pessimistic Concurrency
SELECT FOR UPDATE

During high-demand concert ticket drops, standard database reads risk double-selling. EventSphere applies LockModeType.PESSIMISTIC_WRITE to lock ticket type inventory rows at the MySQL engine level, guaranteeing strict atomic seat decrements.

10-Min Cart Hold & TTL
@Scheduled(60s)

When an attendee selects tickets, the system provisionally holds them with a 10-minute TTL. The background BookingExpiryScheduler runs every 60s, releasing abandoned tickets back to the public pool so seats are never locked indefinitely.

Anti-Tamper QR Passes
HMAC-SHA256

Admission passes do not expose raw sequential IDs. Instead, TicketSigningUtil generates URL-safe tokens encoded as Base64Url(ticketCode:signature). Gate scanners pre-verify signatures in memory before querying the DB.

PayHere IPG Pipeline
MD5 Webhook Sig

Checkouts are secured using SHA-256 merchant hashes. Asynchronous server-to-server webhook callbacks (/api/v1/payments/notify) validate PayHere’s MD5 signature, idempotently confirm bookings, issue tickets, and log audit payloads.

Inbox Deliverability (No Spam)
Brevo Relay + @Async

Standard consumer SMTP (like Gmail) frequently routes cloud-sent emails straight into Spam folders. EventSphere utilizes Brevo’s authenticated transactional SMTP relay with SPF/DKIM alignment, guaranteeing that 6-digit OTPs, itemized receipts, and QR passes land directly in the attendee's Primary Inbox rather than Spam.

Gemini Function Calling
Read-Only Guardrails

Powered by Google Gemini API with native tool calling (search_events, get_event_details, get_my_bookings). Safety guardrails keep the assistant strictly read-only, preventing unauthorized state alterations.

Trust, Security & Data Privacy

Our commitment to safeguarding attendee data and financial transactions

BCrypt Credential Protection

User credentials and passwords are encrypted using BCrypt with a cost factor of 12 before persistence. Personal attendee information is never shared or sold to third parties.

PCI-DSS Payment Isolation

Card payments are handled entirely on PayHere's hosted, PCI-DSS certified gateway. EventSphere never stores or processes credit card numbers, relying on secure tokenized notifications.

Anti-Forgery Admission Verification

Every ticket pass contains a secret HMAC-SHA256 signature. Upon scanning at the gate, the pass transitions to USED instantly with audit logging to prevent reuse.
